Northstar Bank (fictional learning data only)

Overdraft fee: $20 per overdraft item, with a maximum of two overdraft fees per day.

Debit card replacement: Customers may request a standard replacement card for free or an expedited replacement card for $15. Delivery timing is not specified in this FAQ.

Domestic transfer: A domestic bank transfer is usually completed within one business day.

Debit card dispute: Report an unauthorized debit card transaction within 60 calendar days after the statement date.
